# Obby Card Game 2.0 — Rules and Decisions, update 14 Nov 2026

Rulings from the wording pass (Gandalf + Claude, 14 Nov 2026). This doc **overrides `Obby Card Game 2.0 - Rules and Decisions.md` and the other older docs where they differ**. Every card text, cost and Mosje ability is in `Obby Card Game 2.0 - Wording Pass.md` (repo: `docs/obby-2.0/wording-pass.md`).

## New rules
1. **Cost first.** A price before a semicolon is paid when the card is played ("One of your Mosjes loses 10 MP; it gets +20 Power this turn.").
2. **MP costs.** You can't pay an MP cost that would take a Mosje to 0 MP or less.
3. **Dice cap.** A Quest roll never uses more than 6 dice. This includes Personal Quest rolls.
4. **Personal Quest protection.** A Personal Quest can't be discarded by an opponent's effect.
5. **One pile per player.** The Welloe pile and the discard pile are the same pile. Cards say "Welloe pile" when they are about Mosjes and "discard pile" otherwise.
6. **Ready gear.** Gear attached to a Mosje counts as a "ready gear Piecie", for Needs lines and for Pay lines.
7. **Target.** "Target" is a defined word. Piecies, Snelle and Mosje abilities target; attacks don't. Jensen!, Zonnebril, Blensen! and The Protector work against anything that says Target.
8. **Ctrl+S.** A Mosje that can't drop a level and reaches 0 MP stays at 0 and keeps its level. After the protection ends it only drops when it loses MP again.
9. **Level upgrades** on Mosje abilities start at Level 2 ("From Level 2: …").
10. **Upkeep (new duration).** The Protector is a Snelle that attaches at any time and stays: at the start of your turn, pay 1 Energy or discard it. It counts as one of the Mosje's 2 attachments.
11. **Mosje Reborn** can summon a Mosje from any player's pile. You need a free Mosje spot; the Mosje is yours while it is in play (friend bonuses, Personal Quests) and goes back to its owner's pile when it leaves.
12. **Start MP is never 0.** Gandoe, The Destroyer and Alyssa, The Bulldozer start at 10 MP.
13. **Extra uses still pay.** Rebouleh and Youri's ability pay the normal cost of what they activate.
14. **Knocked Down, Not Out:** an attack, a failed Quest or a Pay all count as losing MP.
15. **Binti's cooldown:** her 20 MP hit can't be used on two turns in a row; mark it on the card.
16. **Zwoele Ming Power:** a Quest always needs at least 1 success.
17. **DJ 80/20 counts as a Gandoe.** ★★★★★ cards are limit 1 per deck, and the limit is not printed on the card.

## Card text (final)
- **All text, no glyphs.** Colon = condition, semicolon = cost, "you can" = optional. Durations are fixed phrases: "this turn", "until the end of your next turn", "during your opponent's next turn".
- The word **"die"** in effect text. The plain die icon appears only in Quest and Personal Quest Roll lines.
- Type gates are a **Needs:** line ("Needs: a Digital Mosje."). Star gates remain only as a single condition (Tech ★★ on Counter Strikka and Jammertje Gepakt!, Soc ★★ on Bank Chilling, Res ★★ on Zo is Natuur).
- Friend bonus, three forms: "A <Friend> …" · "With a <Friend> on your field: …" · "On a <Friend>: …" (attached gear).
- Keywords: Needs, Attach, Entrance, Discarded, Level Down, Combo.
