# Obby Card Game 2.0 — Phase 4: Places

Done 2026-10-10. Uses `Obby Card Game 2.0 - Core Numbers.md` (§3 cost curve). Source: the 17 V4 Places plus the 4 Places that only exist in the web game (your call: Boxing Ring, Tesla, Eendjes Voeren, De Box). Result: **20 Places.** 1 parked (Momentum Factory, your call).

## Rules used in this doc (your calls this phase)
- **Card pool:** all 17 V4 Places + the 4 web-only Places − Momentum Factory = **20**.
- **Names:** every name stays, Dutch ones included. Where V4 and the web spell a Place slightly differently, the shorter web spelling is used (Coert's Caravan, Obby #1, Drain Zone, Synergy Chamber).
- **Eendjes Voeren** is the Place. The Piecie that the web game also calls "Eendjes voeren" goes back to its V4 name **Nature's Gift** (Phase 3 doc updated).
- **Which effect:** V4 and the web often give a Place a different effect. I picked whichever fits 2.0 best (your call: "you decide, prefer simpler effects, it's fine if some cards do the same thing"). The other version is in the change note, so you can overrule it.
- **Playing a Place:** on your turn, from your hand, only when no Place is in play. Pay its Energy cost and put it in the middle. It works at once. It needs no Piecie slot. Not on a cooldown turn (Core Numbers §1a).
- **It stays until destroyed** (rule 9 kept). It belongs to the player who played it; when it is destroyed it goes to that player's discard pile. Destroyers: Slecht Gezet, Bong Hit Demolition, Shhh, popo komt!, Huisbaas. Chillingsvoorbij! takes it back to your hand. Tesla also destroys itself (see the card).
- **Rules for everyone:** a Place's text applies to every Mosje on the table, yours and your opponent's. "Good for / Bad for" says who that ends up helping or hurting.
- **Fresh Mosjes ignore Places** (your call): a fresh Mosje gets neither the good nor the bad side of any Place until the start of its owner's next turn.
- **What a Place changes:** Power (±10), Quest dice (+1 die, max 4, or a reroll), Quest MP, Energy costs or card draw. Only The Gym keeps a turn-start MP loss (it is the Rules-doc sample card). No new rule about MP drains this phase (your call: "ignore for now"); it is a Phase 5 flag.
- **Power never goes below 0.** An attack with 0 Power does nothing.
- **Named lines** ("a Gandoe", "Coerts") work for any Mosje with that name, whoever owns it.
- **★★★★★ = limit 1 per deck** (Core Numbers §3).

## Flags for later phases
1. **The Gym's drain and getemt.** A non-Fighting Mosje at 0 MP (for example right after levelling up) is getemt by The Gym at the start of its owner's turn. Left as is ("ignore for now"). If it feels bad in Phase 5: skip Mosjes at 0 MP, or turn it into −10 Power.
2. **The first Place locks the table.** Rule 9 kept: a Place stays until destroyed. Phase 5 starter decks should each carry 1–2 destroyers. Track how often one Place stays all game.
3. **The Void** turns off most MP Piecies for both players. Watch it in Phase 5.
4. **Momentum Stabilizer vs Level 3:** it caps big hits at 30, so a Level 3 Mosje with a little MP left is safer. Track Level 3 holds.
5. **Delluft vs Cless decks:** a Cless deck pays 2 more on its first payment every turn. Harsh on purpose (parking anxiety), but limit 1 per deck. Watch it.
6. **Eendjes Voeren** makes every Mosje Resilient ★★★ on Quests, so the Fighting stack's Resilient Quests get easy for everyone.
7. **Fresh Mosjes ignore Places:** a fresh Mosje doesn't get The Gym's +1 die either. Intended (your call), but players may forget it.
8. **Web game drift (Phase 6 handoff):** the web game hides Drain Zone and The Void from players; both are in the 2.0 pool. The web Piecie "Eendjes voeren" must be renamed back to Nature's Gift. Momentum Factory leaves the pool.
9. **Count:** 20 Places but only one in play at a time. Phase 5 decks should hold 1–3 each.
