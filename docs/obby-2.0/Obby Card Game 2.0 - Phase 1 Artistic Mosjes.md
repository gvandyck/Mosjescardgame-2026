# Obby Card Game 2.0 — Phase 1: Artistic Mosjes

Done 2026-10-09. Uses `Obby Card Game 2.0 - Core Numbers.md` (§4 Mosje stat ranges) and the same format as the Fighting and Digital docs.

## Rules used in this doc
- **Summon cost** is Energy. Power at LVL 1; +10 per level. LVL 3 = hold to win.
- **Traits** printed per level row; V4 traits are the LVL 1 row. Artistic main trait is **Creative**, so Creative climbs first wherever it is below ★★★; when Creative is already ★★★ the Mosje climbs on its side trait, or gains a new one.
- **Activated abilities cost Energy, never MP.** "Once per game" and "N times per game" became once per turn + Energy where possible (nothing hidden on the table).
- **Synergy label:** a Mosje prints only `Synergy: <card name>`. The named card holds the full text. Where two Mosjes named each other, exactly **one** holds the text.
- **Getemt:** any MP loss at 0 MP drops the Mosje a level. Jisca's own failed combo is the one exception (V4 already said "if at 0 MP ignore damage").
- **Chained activations do not chain again (your ruling):** a Piecie activated for free by Jisca, DDR Chris, Ming Natural or Master Plan never starts another chain roll or a Teaching Moment roll. Only a Piecie you activate yourself does.
- Welloe pile = out of the game. "One of your Mosjes" replaces "active Mosje".

## Flags (check in Phase 5 / carry to Phase 2–3)
1. **Roster is 10, not 14.** Three cards are parked (Binti Creator, Amplifier, Kast-elein). Decks in Phase 5 have only 10 Artistic Mosjes to pick from; with 8 Fighting and 14 Digital the Artistic starter deck is the thinnest.
2. **Names owed:** none of the 10 is a placeholder, but the parked Amplifier has no name.
3. **Master Plan back to discard** means a finisher can be replayed every turn for 2 Energy. Check in Phase 5 (Harde Didde, Klaar Met Jou, Dikke Taks). A "Welloe pile after use" fallback was offered.
4. **Chain engine:** Jisca (1 chain), DDR Chris (2 chains), Ming Natural and Master Plan all give free activations. With the "chained activations don't chain" rule the max per turn is bounded, but one self-activated Piecie can still start Jisca + DDR together. Test on paper.
5. **Teaching Moment at +1:** the Teacher/Historian text makes the roll 50% (4–6). Check it isn't too much draw with Time Control's end-of-turn "draw 2".
6. ~~**Bowie & Stormey (Phase 3)**~~ written in the Phase 3 doc (pets). Was: must list Tuk (Healing), Tuk (Sims Architect), Michelle, DJ 80/20 and Gandoe (V4 text names Gandoe/DJ/Tuk/Michelle). Binti Sharp Tongue/KasteLuck/Cless Teacher/DDR need no pet Piecie.
7. ~~**ViannaPoes (Phase 3)**~~ written in the Phase 3 doc: names AZN Cless and Cless, The Teacher (Hayabusa isn't a 2.0 Mosje). Tony (pet) now names Coert, KasteLuck.
8. **Artistic stack lean:** Creative is already ★★★ on 6 of 10 Mosjes, so L2/L3 climbs go to Soc/Men/Res. Phase 2 must give Social real Quests or those climbs are wasted (same issue as Mental in the Digital doc).
9. **Getemt at 0:** Jisca is the only exception. Precision Driver's Pit Stop is safe, Cutting Words and the Binti-Sharp targets are not.
10. **Rarity column is a proposal** from the Core Numbers guide, ignoring the web game's current re-tiering.
