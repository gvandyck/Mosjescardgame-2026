> **Phase 5 update (2026-10-10):** every Mosje got **10 less Power on every level** (Phase 5 doc, R3). The Power column below is already updated; Power numbers inside the change notes are the old ones.

## Rules used in this doc
- **Summon cost** is Energy. Power at LVL 1; +10 per level. LVL 3 = hold to win.
- **Traits** printed per level row; V4 traits are the LVL 1 row. Digital main trait is **Technical**, so Technical climbs first wherever it is below ★★★; after that the side trait the Mosje already leans on.
- **Activated abilities cost Energy, never MP** (MP is the score, not a currency). A "once every 5 turns" or "3 times per game" limit became "once per turn + Energy", so nothing on the table needs a hidden counter.
- **Synergy label:** a Mosje prints only `Synergy: <card name>`. The named card holds the full text. Where two Mosjes named each other in V4, exactly **one** holds the text (see "Synergy text printed here").
- **Getemt:** any MP loss at 0 MP drops the Mosje a level (Fighting ruling), so "lose 10 MP" costs and misses are real risks at 0.
- Welloe pile = out of the game. "One of your Mosjes" replaces "active Mosje".

## Flags (check in Phase 5 / carry to Phase 2)
1. **Mental-heavy roster.** 8 of 14 have Mental ★★★ and Technical ★–★★, but the Digital Quest stack tests Technical. That is why Technical climbs first in the level paths. Phase 2 must give Mental real Quests (side requirement) or these Mosjes have a weak home.
2. **Ronald has Technical ★ only from L2.** At L1 he rolls 1 die (minimum) on Digital Quests.
3. **Names owed:** "[...] The Hacker", "Placeholder 1 The Tactician", "Placeholder 4 The Drainer" need real names.
4. **Info abilities** (Ronald, FPS West, FPS Coert synergy, Senor West) all look at hidden info; test on paper that checking hands doesn't slow the table.
5. **Tactician combo:** set an opponent's Mosje to 0, then any MP loss to it counts as getemt (a level drop) — 2 Energy plus a cheap attack. Check on paper whether getemt at a temporary 0 should count, or whether temporary MP is immune to getemt. Also needs a table marker for "old MP" (e.g. leave the real MP marker, slide a second one).
6. **Getemt at 0:** Senor West wrong guess, FPS West wrong guess and Gandoe's own rolls hurt at 0 MP. Same risk as the Fighting flag 1.
7. ~~Synergy text owed on Artistic cards~~ — settled in `Phase 1 Artistic Mosjes`: Binti, The Sharp Tongue holds the Coert Savant text only (KasteLuck and Kast-elein dropped it); Cless, The Teacher pairs with Martin, The Historian (text above); Youri pairs with Dancing/DDR Chris only and Chris, The All-Rounder lost the Youri label. FPS West no longer pairs with Cless.
8. **Youri's per-game limit is gone:** with 1 Energy per use it can fire every turn. Checked in Phase 3: no clash with the Snelle slot rule. Youri's Piecies are still placed in a slot and only work on your own turn; Snelle are the only cards that work on the opponent's turn.
