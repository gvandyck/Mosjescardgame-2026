# Obby Card Game 2.0 — Phase 3: Piecies & Snelle

Done 2026-10-10. Uses `Obby Card Game 2.0 - Core Numbers.md` (§3 cost curve, §5 MP → Energy). Source: the V4 Piecies and Snelle plus the cards that only exist in the web game (your call). Result: **73 Piecies + 20 Snelle = 93 cards.** 1 card cut (MP Adjuster), 1 left out (Buurvrouw, empty in V4 and not in the web game).

## Rules used in this doc (your calls this phase)
- **Card pool:** all V4 Piecies/Snelle + 9 web-only Piecies (Loaded Dice, Boosterpackkie, Perfect Rhythm, Dikke Plaat, Kan het?!, Dumbbells, Boxing Gloves, Skipping Rope, Protein Shake) + the web-only Snelle Chillingsvoorbij!.
- **Names:** the web game's names where it renamed a V4 card (table at the end). Exception: Lucky Cóin keeps the spelling of the 2.0 sample card.
- **Text base:** the web text (newer), except V4 for **Lucky Cóin, Jammertje Gepakt!, Perfect Dodge, Jensen! → Frenssen! → Blensen!** and **Counter Strikka** (V4 redirect, so it isn't a twin of Jammertje). Where a web text breaks a ruling below (Emergency Healings, Welloe Force, Drain Reversal, Je Weet Niet), it was reworked.
- **Cost = Energy**, from the Phase 0 table and checked against the budget `25 + 12 × Energy` MP. Piecies may carry the full value (they pay a slot and a turn of waiting); Snelle are worth about 10 MP less than their cost because they're instant. Deviations from the table are noted per card.
- **Attacks are mostly Power boosts:** "one of your Mosjes gets +X Power this turn". The Mosje still has to attack, which is its one action. Only a few cards make a Mosje lose MP directly, and only small amounts (10–15): Affoe, Straffoe, Kleine Taks, Varkenspootjes, Jantje Jantje... Jantje?, Battle Concert, Drain Reversal, Welloe Force, Frenssen!.
- **Shields reduce, they never stop:** "loses X less", never "can't lose MP". Welloe protection (Mosje Shield, Not Today!) is fine because it isn't about MP.
- **A loss reduced to 0 is no loss:** the Mosje is not getemt, also at 0 MP. So a shield can save a Level 3 Mosje from a weak attack, not from a strong one.
- **Your own costs and drawbacks are MP loss too** (Phase 1 ruling): Warm Kannetje Melk, Te Hard Gaan, Straffoe and friends getemt a Mosje at 0 MP. Pick a Mosje that has the MP.
- **Fresh Mosje = untouchable:** until the start of its owner's next turn no opponent card, Quest jab or ability can choose it (not only attacks).
- **Level gate:** only **Harde Didde, Klaar Met Jou and Dikke Taks** keep "Needs: one of your Mosjes at Level 2+". "Lvl 1+" means nothing now (no Level 0) and was dropped everywhere.
- **Quest boosts:** "+1 die on one Quest this turn" (max 4 dice), the Phase 2 wording. Old "+2 to the roll" cards give +1 die **and** a reroll of 1 die.
- **Tags:** food, pet, substance, gear. One tag per card, printed after the type: `Piecie · food`. See "Tags" below.
- **Lasting cards ("Stays"):** the card stays face-up in its slot (it still uses the slot) until the time it says, then goes to the discard pile. Default: until the end of your next turn. A Piecie that stays is what Endure Pain now asks for.
- **Snelle slot rule:** a Snelle needs a free Piecie slot. It goes into that slot, resolves and goes to the discard pile (Blensen! stays until the end of the turn as a reminder). With 3 Piecies in play you can't play a Snelle. Snelle played on the opponent's turn use your saved Energy. No Snelle during your cooldown turn (Core Numbers §1a).
- **Piecies from your hand** (Dubbele Ding) need a free slot too, like a Snelle.
- **Chains** (Phase 1 ruling): a Piecie activated for free never starts another chain roll or Teaching Moment roll. Copies made by Dubbele Temminks or Gevalletje Klakkeloos are not activations.
- **"Free" = 0 Energy. Boosts last "this turn".** "Your next Quest" became "a Quest this turn".
- **★★★★★ = limit 1 per deck** (Core Numbers §3). Other limits are printed on the card.
- **Named bonus:** only while that Mosje is on your field. A Piecie that holds a Mosje's synergy text starts it with "While <name> is also on your field:" (Phase 1 convention).
- Two players: "all opponents" became "your opponent". "Graveyard" became discard pile; the Welloe pile is out of the game.

## Flags for later phases
1. **Ronald Kip without the Level 2 gate.** With Ronald, The Master Chef on your field it gives +60 and a card for 2 Energy. A cost-2 Mosje with 40 starting MP can level up on turn 3. Watch it in Phase 5.
2. **Shield stacking on a Level 3.** Afblijven! (10), a pet (10 / 20), The Protector (30), Perfect Dodge (20 / 30), Drain Reversal (20), Emergency Healings (+25 first), Momentum Rush (+15) and Bijna Welloe (+20) can stack on one Mosje. Each reduces, but three together can beat a low-Power deck. Track Level 3 holds in Phase 5.
3. **Jensen! (1 Energy) cancels a kill card (4 Energy).** That is the counter chain working as intended (Frenssen! answers it), but watch it.
4. **Snelle slot rule:** three Piecies in play block all your Snelle. Check in Phase 5 whether players keep a slot free, and whether that feels good.
5. **Lots of 0-Energy Piecies (28).** Check in Phase 5 that Energy doesn't pile up unused at the cap of 6.
6. **Leipe Swap + attack:** swap your 0-MP Mosje with their 95, then attack their 0-MP Mosje and it is getemt. Limit 1 per deck; watch it.
7. **MP Amplifier + Ronald Kip** = +90 MP from two cards (3 Energy), +100 with Ronald, The Master Chef. The Coert Savant food bonus was made flat to stop a jump like this; this combo needs 2 slots and 2 turns of setup. Watch it.
8. **Kill cards:** Harde Didde, Klaar Met Jou (1 per deck each) and Gandoe, The Destroyer. Answers: Not Today!, Mosje Shield, Jensen! and Counter Strikka.
9. **Phase 4 hooks:** Boxing Ring (old Protein Shake bonus), Bank Chilling (Jantje Jantje... Jantje?), Dierenasiel (pets), Digital Gaming Stop (gear; check whether it should count sport gear too), Delluft, Coert's Explosive Caravan. Four Place destroyers exist (Slecht Gezet, Bong Hit Demolition, Shhh popo komt!, Huisbaas), which feeds Alyssa, Fissa Fissa!.
10. **Phase 2 wording to settle:** Dutch courage and Larry Temmen say "discard 1 substance Piecie" without "ready" or "from your hand"; Endurance Test says "ready". Pick one wording in Phase 5 or 6.
11. **Cheat code** (Digital Quest) discards a Snelle; Digital starter decks need a few (Phase 5).
12. **Web game drift:** the web game already has tags (FOOD, PET, SUBSTANCE, DIGITAL-EQUIPMENT, PHYSICAL-EQUIPMENT). In the Phase 6 handoff the two equipment tags become one `gear` tag, and every text in this doc replaces the web text.
13. **Rarity column is a proposal** that follows the cost curve, not the web game's current tiers.
