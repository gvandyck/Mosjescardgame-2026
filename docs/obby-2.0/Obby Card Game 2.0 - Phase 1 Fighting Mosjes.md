> **Phase 5 update (2026-10-10):** every Mosje got **10 less Power on every level** (Phase 5 doc, R3). The Power column below is already updated; Power numbers inside the change notes are the old ones.

## Rules used in this doc
- **Summon cost** is Energy. Power at LVL 1; +10 per level. LVL 3 = hold to win.
- **Traits** printed per level row; V4 traits are the LVL 1 row, one trait climbs one star per level.
- **Synergy label** (your decision): a Mosje prints only `Synergy: <card name>`. The named card holds the full text of what happens while both are on your field. The Mosje itself carries no synergy text.
- **Getemt** (your decision): any MP loss at 0 MP makes a Mosje getemt, not only opponent attacks. This includes failed Quests and its own ability costs/rolls. See flags below.
- Welloe pile = out of the game. "One of your Mosjes" replaces "active Mosje".

## Flags (check in Phase 5)
1. **Any MP loss at 0 = getemt** (your ruling) combined with level-up resetting to 0: right after levelling, one failed Quest or a bad Chaos Roll drops the Mosje a level. Expect Quests to feel risky after every level-up; watch if players stop questing at 0 MP.
2. **0-MP starters** (Alyssa Bulldozer, Gandoe Destroyer) are getemt on their first failed Quest. That's the cost of their Power 30; test it.
3. **Gandoe Wizard's Chaos Roll** at 0 MP: a 1–2 makes him getemt. Chaos is on-brand, but it's a 33% self-sabotage per turn at 0 MP.
