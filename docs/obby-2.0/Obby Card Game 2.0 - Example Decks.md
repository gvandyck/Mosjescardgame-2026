# Obby Card Game 2.0 — Example Decks

Phase 6 output, 2026-10-10. The three starter decks from Phase 5 in their final form. Card texts are in `Obby Card Game 2.0 - Card List.md`; why each card is in its deck is in the Phase 5 doc.

## Deck rules
- **30 cards**, Quests not included (Quests are the 3 shared stacks in the middle).
- At most **2 copies** of a card; ★★★★★ cards at most 1.
- Every deck: **6 Mosjes, 2 Places, 18 Piecies (2 of them Place destroyers), 4 Snelle**, one Mosje type.

## Setup (both players)
1. Take your deck's **starting Mosje** out of the deck and put it on your field. It is free and fresh.
2. Shuffle the other 29 cards and draw 3.
3. Each player starts with 2 Energy. Shuffle the three Quest stacks and turn the top card of each face-up.
4. Roll a die each; highest goes first. **Player 1 skips their draw on their first turn.**
