# Playtesting Guide

## Purpose
This guide is for running human playtests with the current engine state after Phase 10 and the Phase 11 documentation pass.

Current baseline from simulation (100 games):
- Crashes: 0
- Timeout rate: 28%
- Never-played cards: 21
- Average game length: 27.63 turns

## Game Setup
1. Use one of the three starter decks only:
- Physical Force
- Digital Control
- Artistic Rhythm
2. Seat 2 players and assign one deck each.
3. Use standard starting Mosjes from each starter list.
4. Keep a simple note sheet with:
- Matchup
- Winner and win condition
- Turn count
- Rules confusion moments
- Cards that felt too strong/too weak
5. For first-time sessions, do not mix in booster-heavy or advanced cards.

## Turn Order (Human Table Flow)
1. Start turn effects and draw.
2. Main actions:
- Play Piecies
- Play at most 1 Place per turn
- Use Mosje ability if legal
3. Attempt Quest if desired.
4. End turn and resolve turn-end effects.

## Starter Deck Profiles

### Physical Force
Simple pressure deck. Gain MP quickly and convert into direct attacks. Best for players who like straightforward plans.

### Digital Control
Consistency and denial deck. Draw/filter tools plus disruption. Best for players who like resource control and timing.

### Artistic Rhythm
Tempo/synergy deck. Chains medium-value cards and turn sequencing. Best for players who like combo pacing.

## First-Time Explanation Script (Plain Language)
Use this language before game 1:
- Your MP is your energy bar. Most cards either gain MP, drain MP, or convert MP into board advantage.
- You mainly win by leveling your active Mosje to level 3, so your turn is usually about efficient MP flow and safe pressure.
- Piecies are your normal play cards. Snelle Piecies are fast response cards that interrupt or counter.
- Places are field cards with ongoing effects. Only one active place matters at a time.
- Quests are optional risk/reward actions. If your deck is new to you, treat quests as bonus value, not your whole plan.

## Test Session Format
1. Run best-of-3 per matchup.
2. Swap decks after game 1 where possible.
3. Force at least one game where each player attempts a quest by turn 4.
4. Track if game pace stalls after turn 20.
5. Mark any card text that needed judge clarification.

## Feedback Questions (Ask After Each Match)
1. Which turn felt like the momentum swing, and why?
2. Did you understand when to attempt a quest?
3. Did Place effects feel clear and visible?
4. Did any Snelle interaction feel unfair or confusing?
5. Which card felt strongest in your match?
6. Which card felt weakest or dead in hand?
7. Did you ever feel stuck with no useful play?
8. Was the game length too long, too short, or acceptable?
9. Were the deck themes (Physical/Digital/Artistic) distinct enough?
10. What one rule or card text should be simplified first?
