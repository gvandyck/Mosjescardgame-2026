Never on a card
  • Type pills (PIECIE, SNELLE, PLACE, QUEST)
  • Rarity diamonds or any rarity mark
  • STAYS chip (the card’s own “Stays …” sentence is enough)
  • Rules sentences in the card text that the rulebook covers (e.g. “play any time, also on your opponent’s turn”)
  • Level-up, MP reset, “hold to win” text
  • Corner glyphs or a level badge
  • A text box behind the description
  • Boxed art windows or tier 1–3 frames
  • Separate level cards (the level is a marker on the table)
  • A Personal Quest printed on a Mosje (it is its own card)
  • Fail pips on Quests (a Quest leaves after one attempt)
Card type colours
The frame line is a 2 px line 8 px inside the card edge, broken around the side labels. The tint colours only the small labels (COST, POWER, MP, NEED, WIN, LOSE). All other text, keywords and both side labels included, is white. No other colour on a card.

Mosje type colours
Mosjes have no frame line. Their type colour shows only in the tint of the small labels (COST / MP / LV1–3 / POWER · TRAITS) and the trait stars.
