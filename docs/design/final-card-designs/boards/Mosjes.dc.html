Rules on this board
  • One card per Mosje. Summon it (cost top-left); it starts at Level 1 with the printed MP (default 10).
  • Level up: at 100 MP move the level marker up, MP back to 10. Power and traits come from that level’s line on the strip.
  • Getemt at Level 2/3: drop one level, MP back to 10. No card is lost.
  • Ultimate: from hand on that Mosje: free at Level 3, 3 Energy at Level 1–2. Replaces Power and traits; level and MP keep working underneath. Getemt: the Ultimate is discarded, the Mosje keeps its level at 10 MP.
  • Personal Quest: its own card. Played from hand by a Level 3 Mosje of that type, as its action. Win: you win the game. Fail: the cards it needed are discarded; the PQ goes back into your deck (reshuffle). Every deck needs at least 1 PQ.
  • Left side label: OBBY CARD GAME on Mosjes again (the level is no longer a card), ULTIMATE on Ultimates.
Ultimate and Personal Quest cardsUltimate: full art foil, own artwork, ★★★★★, limit 1, cost 3 or free at Level 3. Personal Quest: gold line #D4A017, tint #FEF3C7, top-left = the level it needs; left label = which Mosje type may attempt it.
