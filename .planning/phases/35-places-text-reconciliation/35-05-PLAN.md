<objective>
Rework Skiffa from its current "discard 1 OR lose 15 MP" theme (the discard-choice branch was
never built — a code comment admits the stub) into the ruled new design: a flat +2 dice-roll
bonus for any player attempting a Social-category quest while Skiffa is active. Also remove an
undocumented third mechanic this phase's research uncovered (`getSkiffaRerolls`) that predates
and is unrelated to both the old and new Skiffa themes.

Purpose: Skiffa's card text ("discard 1 card OR lose 15 MP, player choice") was never actually
implemented as a choice — the engine always applies -15 to non-SUBSTANCE Mosjes. Rather than
build the never-requested discard-choice UI, CONTEXT.md's ruling replaces the whole card with a
Social-quest dice bonus, reusing the exact stacking pattern Synergy Chamber's own dice bonus
already uses in `main.js`. Research separately found `getSkiffaRerolls` — an ARTISTIC-Mosje
reroll grant with zero textual basis in either the old or new Skiffa design — sitting undetected
next to the discard/-15 code; it must be disposed of in the same commit so it doesn't silently
stack with the new +2 bonus. This is one of the MP-touching cards (PLACE-10) per CLAUDE.md's
MP-calculation caution rule — even though the change is to a dice-roll bonus rather than a direct
MP amount, it changes the effective success rate of every Social quest, which is downstream of
MP economy — so both tasks re-run the Ronald Kip stacking test and full simulation.
Output: `place_skiffa.trigger` becomes `ON_QUEST`; `effect_skiffa` (the old discard/-15 body) and
its dispatcher `case` are removed; `src/main.js` gains a Social-quest `+2` term in the existing
dice-bonus sum at both quest-attempt call sites; `getSkiffaRerolls` and its 4 call sites are
deleted.
</objective>
