option (D-02) and no 20 MP is charged when unaffordable.

then fixed, then the same spec passes. Personal Quests (already gated, D-05) get a regression
test in the same spec (D-01 "both quest types").

changing the canonical 20 MP fee or its lethality (phase0-rulings.md:126).
Output: a new real-engine repro/regression spec + a one-branch fix in src/main.js.
</objective>
