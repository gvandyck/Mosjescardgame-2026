<objective>
Rewire the lobby around the active-deck concept: signed-in users get an active-deck panel + a
Change-deck switcher modal; guests get a dropdown of the 5 duo decks only; game start reads the
active deck (signed in) or dropdown (guest); the bot picks a true-random duo deck. All three
surfaces share the single getPlayerFacingDecks() accessor, and no default falls back to an original.

Purpose: Close the loop from onboarding (34-02) to actual play — the player's chosen/active deck
drives every game, and originals never appear to players.
Output: a pure bot-deck picker, an active-deck panel component, lobby wiring, unit + Playwright tests.
</objective>
