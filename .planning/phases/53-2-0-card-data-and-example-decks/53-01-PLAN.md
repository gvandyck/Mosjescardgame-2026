  - mosje row: `{ name, section: 'FIGHTING'|'DIGITAL'|'ARTISTIC', cost: Number, rarity: '★..', startMP: Number, levels: parseLevelCell(...), abilities: [{ name, text }], holderText: string|null, synergyCell: string|null }`. Name: strip ` *(name owed)*`, keep everything else verbatim (incl. literal `[...]`). Ability cell: split on `<br>`; a segment starting `**While ` becomes `holderText` with the `**` removed (e.g. `While Dancing/DDR Chris is also on your field: you may ...`); other segments `**Name** — text` become `{ name, text }` (text verbatim after ` — `, `**` stripped). Synergy `—` -> null.
  - quest row: `{ name, stack: 'FIGHTING'|'DIGITAL'|'ARTISTIC' (from the stack heading), band: snake id steady|skilled|heroic|prepared|gated|coin_flip|trained, rolls: raw cell, costText: string|null, win, lose, extras: string|null }`.

  - piecie row: `{ name, group: 'mp'|'attack'|'utility'|null (gear/pet/substance sections -> null), tag: 'food'|'pet'|'substance'|'gear'|null (from Kind after ' · '), cost: Number, rarity, text }`. For the Pet section, `text` = the shared pet line (bold sentence above the Pet table, `**` stripped) + ' ' + the row text. Strip `**` from all texts.
  - snelle row: `{ name, cost: Number, costText: string|null, rarity, text }`; the only non-numeric cost `4 or free` -> `cost: 4, costText: '4 or free'`; any other non-integer cost throws.
  - place row: `{ name, cost, rarity, text, goodFor: string[], badFor: string[] }` (split on `, `; `—` -> []).
