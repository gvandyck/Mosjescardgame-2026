- `src/engine/mp/getPower.js`: `export function getPower(slot)` -> `Math.max(0, roundToTen((getLevelRow(slot.cardId, slot.level)?.power ?? 0) + (Number(slot.powerBoost) || 0)))`. `slot.powerBoost` is the field later phases write boosts into.
