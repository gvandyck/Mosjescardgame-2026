<objective>
Replace the wrong Wave 1+2 Call of the Welloes mechanic with the correct one.

Wrong mechanic (built in Waves 1–2):
- confirmCallOfWelloes restored the Mosje's saved mp/level from the welloe record
- returnMosjeToWelloe silently re-archived the Mosje (no defeat, no victory check)
- The Piecie was swept normally by the end-of-turn loop

Correct mechanic (this plan):
- confirmCallOfWelloes always summons at Level 1, 50 MP
- When the anchor Piecie is gone at end of turn, markMosjeDefeated is called on the linked Mosje (it is truly defeated, not silently returned)
- The piecie_call_of_welloes Piecie is persistent while its linked Mosje is alive; it is only swept when linkedMosjeCardId is absent or the linked Mosje is no longer in activeSlots
- markMosjeDefeated clears linkedMosjeCardId on the Piecie slot so the Piecie can be swept the following turn

Purpose: The Piecie's power is balanced around a fresh summon (Lvl 1, 50 MP) and the risk that destroying the Piecie truly defeats the summoned Mosje (triggering defeat side-effects including victory check). The Wave 1+2 implementation inverted this — it made the card too strong (stat restoration) and removed the penalty for Piecie destruction (silent return).

Output: Corrected engine functions, corrected description, corrected tests. All 896+ tests green.
</objective>
