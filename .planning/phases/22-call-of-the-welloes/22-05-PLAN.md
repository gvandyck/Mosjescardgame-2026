<objective>
Close the bidirectional-destroy gap in the Call of the Welloes mechanic.

The Piecie lingers until the end-of-turn sweep on the NEXT turn, which is incorrect. The correct mechanic: when the summoned Mosje dies, the Piecie is immediately discarded in the same engine call.

Purpose: Make destruction truly bidirectional — either card dying removes both from the field at the same moment.
Output: One new failing test (L) that proves the gap, then a ~5-line code change in victoryChecker.js that makes it green.
</objective>
