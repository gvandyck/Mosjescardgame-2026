<objective>
Rework the Artistic Rhythm starter deck to fix the unplayable personal quest, remove dead-weight cards, and create a coherent DJ 8020 + AZN Cless risk/reward theme. Also fix the isBoosterOnly data inconsistency on all three personal quests and align the simulation deck.

Purpose: Three problems compound in Artistic Rhythm. (1) quest_personal_lucky_crescendo requires place_skiffa to be active — the deck has Quest Haven + Bank Chilling only, so this quest can NEVER fire. (2) piecie_larry_zegeltje has roll 1–2 = -25 MP AND discard 1, which punishes Binti's discard ability — brutal downside in a card-light deck. (3) piecie_slecht_gezet destroys own Quest Haven or Bank Chilling — pure anti-synergy. Binti moved to Digital Control in Plan 15-02, making AZN Cless the natural replacement: both DJ 8020 and AZN Cless have synergyWith pointing toward a shared Martin/DJ chain, and DJ's +2 quest roll bonus synergizes with Cless's risk/reward dice ability. Additionally, all three personal quests have isBoosterOnly: true despite appearing in starter decks — a data inconsistency that must be corrected.

Output: Updated starterDecks.js (ARTISTIC_RHYTHM object), quests.js (isBoosterOnly on 3 personal quests), starter-decks.ts (ARTISTIC_RHYTHM_MOSJES), deck-balance.test.ts (RED-then-GREEN TDD assertions for DECK-09 through DECK-14), and docs/card-reference.md (Phase 15 deck notes).
