<objective>
Rework the Digital Control starter deck to wire the food-double synergy that has been flavor-only since the game launched, remove the anti-synergy card Slecht Gezet, and sharpen the control identity.

Purpose: hasFoodDoubleSynergy() in synergyResolver.js hardcodes mosje_binti + mosje_coert_tech as the food-double pair. Coert Tech is in the current deck, but Binti is absent — so the food-double NEVER fires, and Kannetje Melk gives flat 25 MP instead of 50. Swapping Martin → Binti wires the synergy instantly without any engine changes — the hasFoodDoubleSynergy() function already works correctly when the right pair is fielded.

Output: Updated starterDecks.js (DIGITAL_CONTROL object), starter-decks.ts (DIGITAL_CONTROL_MOSJES), and deck-balance.test.ts (RED-then-GREEN TDD assertions for DECK-06 through DECK-08).
